# Human Lexical and Editorial Review Protocol

Automated validators protect structure, consistency and known rules. They do not establish native-language or pedagogical quality.

## Vocabulary

Every active vocabulary entry must be reviewed by a qualified Persian-speaking English teacher, lexicographer, or equivalent reviewer for:

- lexical sense distinction;
- Persian gloss accuracy and naturalness;
- normalized part of speech;
- inflection/irregular forms where relevant;
- common level-appropriate collocations;
- usage/register notes where relevant;
- pronunciation traps for Persian speakers where relevant;
- English example naturalness;
- Persian example translation;
- ambiguity with synonyms, homographs and homophones.

Polysemous/homographic items are reviewed as senses, not merely strings. Do not add lexical enrichment simply to fill fields; fields may remain absent when they are not useful.

## Stories and assessments

Every current chapter and assessment must receive human review for:

- grammatical and idiomatic English;
- exact/natural Persian correspondence;
- A1 suitability and controlled-vocabulary consistency;
- story continuity and character logic;
- distractor ambiguity;
- cultural fit without distorting ordinary English usage;
- transfer-test independence where applicable.

## Sign-off rule

AI review, automated linting, test coverage and generated checklists never count as human sign-off. The ledger may list a word only after the full checklist has been checked. Final completion requires two genuine sign-offs (vocabulary and chapter/assessment). Each sign-off records reviewer, role, date, scope and the exact active vocabulary SHA-256 so a later deck revision cannot silently inherit approval.
