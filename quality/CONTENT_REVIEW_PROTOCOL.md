# Human Lexical and Editorial Review Protocol

Automated validators protect structure and known rules; they do not establish native-language quality.

For every one of the 899 vocabulary entries, a qualified Persian-speaking English teacher or lexicographer reviews the checklist in `quality/lexical-review.json` and then adds the word id to `reviewedWordIds`. Final completion requires a real reviewer name/role, date and review scope. Never invent or AI-generate sign-off.

Review every chapter and assessment for:

- grammatical naturalness in English;
- exact and natural Persian correspondence;
- story continuity and character logic;
- A1 suitability;
- lexical sense consistency with the assigned word;
- distractor ambiguity;
- cultural fit without distorting ordinary English usage;
- whether examples teach normal usage/collocation rather than merely containing the word.

Homographs and polysemous headwords must be reviewed as senses, not only strings. Irregular forms, collocations, usage notes and Persian-speaker pronunciation traps should be added only after review.

The native-editorial gate closes only after the ledger covers all active words and the current chapter/assessment content has a dated human sign-off.
