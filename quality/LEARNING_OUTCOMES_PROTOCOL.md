# Ghesse Learning-Outcomes Validation Protocol

## Purpose

This protocol turns “best in class” from a design claim into a measurable learning claim. It must be run with real Persian-speaking learners; repository automation cannot mark it complete.

## Population

Recruit at least 30 Persian-speaking adult or teen English learners who are genuinely A1 or near-A1. Record prior English exposure and whether each sampled target was already known. Do not infer proficiency from app completion.

## Primary outcomes

Measure the same sampled vocabulary at:

- immediate post-learning;
- 24 hours;
- 7 days;
- 30 days.

Primary metrics are delayed **typed productive recall** and **listening-to-meaning recall**. Report item-level and learner-level accuracy with confidence intervals.

## Transfer outcomes

Use material not seen during training:

1. new sentences containing learned words;
2. short unseen reading passages;
3. short unseen listening passages;
4. prompted sentence production using target words;
5. optional recorded speaking scored for intelligibility by a blinded human rater.

Course examples must not be reused as transfer-test items.

## Comparator

Use a preregistered within- or between-subject comparator. At minimum compare the adaptive Ghesse path with a simpler teach/retest condition matched for approximate study time. Do not use a no-study control as the only comparator.

## Calibration questions

Estimate these from observed delayed outcomes rather than intuition:

- desired FSRS retention target;
- acquisition interval ceilings;
- the delayed-consolidation minimum;
- success-day requirements for strong/mastered;
- productive-recall requirements;
- daily review load;
- book, midpoint and final exam thresholds.

Do not make thresholds harder merely because a higher number appears more rigorous.

## Data collection

Use the Settings **de-identified research export**. It contains aggregate learning evidence but excludes raw answers, recordings, exact dates, device identifiers and account identifiers. Participant IDs belong in the external study system, not inside Ghesse.

## Analysis

Pre-register exclusions and the primary endpoint. Report retention at every delay, productive versus receptive performance, unseen-context transfer, attrition and uncertainty. Do not tune policy on the final holdout cohort.

## Gate to close

The learner-outcomes gate may change from pending only after a real dated study report is linked. The learning-policy-calibration gate remains pending until policy changes are justified by those observations.
