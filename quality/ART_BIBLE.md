# Ghesse Character and World Bible

This is the art-direction contract for future chapter art. Existing hash/review gates remain authoritative for shipped pixels.

## Signature visual language

Ghesse should read as a contemporary Iranian family mystery, not as a generic AI animation still. New art uses warm observational storytelling, grounded Iranian locations, natural materials and varied cinematic composition. Avoid repeated “several smiling characters centered toward camera” staging.

## Character invariants

**Mina**
- stable apparent age, face proportions, dark hair/eye identity and body proportions;
- contemporary Iranian hijab in the contexts required by the product canon;
- varied plausible contemporary outfits rather than one fixed costume.

**Parents and recurring relatives**
- preserve recognizable face shape, age, hair/facial-hair and body proportions;
- wardrobe may vary while identity remains stable.

**Nino**
- small yellow chicken with the canon-specific identifying details;
- absent from missing-Nino chapters except explicit lookalike/presence overrides.

## World continuity

Maintain references for Mina’s home/street, Tehran market/cafe/school/office, recurring maps/notes/blue-door clue, transport, and the northern/coastal/mountain journey. Time-of-day and weather continuity must follow the chapter text where relevant.

## Composition rotation

Across each book deliberately mix wide establishing scenes, intimate two-person scenes, clue/object closeups, over-the-shoulder investigation, quiet interiors, landscapes, movement/travel and group scenes only when narratively justified.

## Image rules

- single scene, never collage/storyboard/montage;
- no generated readable text unless later composited from verified typography;
- no accidental Nino in missing chapters;
- no anatomy/object errors that change story meaning;
- no character identity drift;
- no visual element contradicting the exact reviewed chapter JSON blob.

## Approval

Technical validity is not sufficient. Human review should confirm identity, story accuracy, Iranian setting, composition distinctiveness and absence of generic/repetitive staging before the visual-art-direction gate is marked complete.
