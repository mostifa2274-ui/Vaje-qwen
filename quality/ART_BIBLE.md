# Ghesse Character and World Bible

This is the visual-direction contract for future chapter artwork. Existing file/hash/story validation remains necessary, but technical validity alone does not close the visual-art-direction gate.

## Signature language

Ghesse is a contemporary Iranian family mystery, not a generic animated-film still. Art should feel grounded in real Iranian domestic and public environments, warm and observational, with recurring visual motifs and deliberate cinematic variation.

Avoid repeated “several smiling characters centered toward camera” staging. Rotate between wide establishing views, intimate two-person scenes, clue/object closeups, over-the-shoulder investigation, quiet interiors, landscapes, movement/travel and group compositions only when the story calls for them.

## Character invariants

**Mina**
- stable apparent age, face proportions, dark hair/eye identity and body proportions;
- contemporary Iranian hijab in contexts required by the product canon;
- varied plausible contemporary outfits rather than one fixed costume;
- no identity drift between chapters.

**Parents and recurring relatives**
- stable face shape, age, hair/facial-hair and body proportions;
- wardrobe may change while identity remains recognizable.

**Nino**
- small yellow chicken with stable identifying features;
- absent from missing-Nino chapters except an explicitly reviewed story requirement.

## World continuity

Maintain consistent references for Mina’s home/street, Tehran locations, recurring clues/objects, transport, and the northern/coastal/mountain journey. Time-of-day, weather, architecture, furniture and props should follow the reviewed chapter text.

## Image rules

- one coherent scene, never collage/storyboard/montage;
- no generated readable text unless later composited from verified typography;
- no anatomy/object defects that alter story meaning;
- no character identity drift;
- no unexplained Nino appearances;
- no visual contradiction of the exact reviewed chapter JSON blob;
- culturally grounded details should remain contemporary Iranian rather than generic “Middle Eastern” shorthand.

## Human approval

A qualified human art director must review the complete active chapter-art set for identity continuity, story accuracy, Iranian setting, composition variety, visual distinctiveness and technical defects. The sign-off must record reviewer, role, date and scope; the status ledger separately binds approval to the exact art-manifest SHA-256 and all 40 reviewed chapter IDs.
